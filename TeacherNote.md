For each of the four topics, the team must complete the following steps:

Step 1: Literature Search

Search for relevant papers from at least three top-tier conferences or journals in the selected research area.

For the identified papers, create a comparison table containing important information, such as: Authors, Paper title, Conference/journal, Publication year, Source code availability and link, Dataset(s), Dataset availability, Hardware/computational requirements, Main techniques/methods, Experimental settings, Key results, Limitations, etc.

The literature search should be comprehensive and systematic, rather than limited to only a few well-known papers.

Step 2: Paper Filtering and Analysis

Based on the literature review, filter the papers and identify the most interesting and potentially feasible research directions.

The team should consider: Difficulty, Required skills, Available datasets, Availability of source code, Computational resources and hardware requirements.

The goal is to identify research problems that are both interesting and realistically achievable by the team.

Step 3: Proposed Research Direction

For each topic, propose what you would like to investigate or develop in this course. For each proposed research direction, formulate one or more hypotheses. The hypotheses should specify what you expect to improve, explain, or discover through your experiments.

4. Use of AI Tools

Students are allowed and encouraged to use AI tools to support this assignment. 

However, AI tools do not replace the students' responsibility for verifying the information. Every detail, statement, dataset, source-code link must be independently verified by the team. Hallucinated, fabricated, or unverified information is not acceptable. Students are responsible for the accuracy and integrity of the final report, regardless of whether the information was generated or assisted by AI.
